Your accounts

No accounts yet. Here's how to start:
  1. In ServiceNow, press Create a new list.
  2. Select a list you already have for your customer, or create one from scratch.
  3. Add a condition: Customer account is your customer.
  4. In Select columns, add the columns you want (more columns give a better review), then press Create.
  5. In the list shown, press Export.
  6. Drop the downloaded file in the box above and name the account (for example acme).
